The idea was simple: same rooftop, same time, same lens, 30 days straight. What I didn't expect was how much it would change the way I see light.

This video walks through the full project — the setup, the surprises, the one day I almost didn't go up and it turned out to be the best sunset of the entire month.

If you're thinking about starting a creative constraint project, this might convince you.
